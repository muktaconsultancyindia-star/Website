About the name

मुक्ता: free, and a pearl

MUKTA (Multi Utility Knowledge & Technical Assistance) is an enterprise consultancy dedicated to rural livelihood systems, grassroots production units, and institutional project design. In Hindi, the name carries two meanings: to be free (मुक्त), and a pearl (मोती). That is the idea behind the firm: help rural families, especially women, become free to earn on their own terms, and build enterprises with lasting value.

The mark shows a pearl held by two leaves, the group and the community around it.

Our multidisciplinary advisory team brings over 15+ years of combined field expertise across Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh in poultry, agri-allied enterprises, and community-led value chains.

PEOPLE | KNOWLEDGE | IMPACTwhat our tagline means
  1. PeopleEvery project starts and ends with people. We sit with the community, the client and every partner, understand their needs and strengths, and plan around them. Women, farmers, producers and field teams are treated as partners in the work, not just beneficiaries of it.
  2. KnowledgeWe bring technical know-how, sound business systems and practical training to the ground. Groups learn to run production, keep accounts and take decisions on their own. The knowledge stays with them long after our team steps back.
  3. ImpactWe measure success by what changes on the ground: steady income for families, real ownership by members and enterprises that keep running after the project ends. Every result is recorded and reported, so clients and funders can see the difference their support has made.
